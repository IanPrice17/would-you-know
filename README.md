# Would You Know? Practice

Sing-along practice for *Would You Know?* (H. C. Chapman, Bowdoin, 1866).

Open **https://ianprice17.github.io/would-you-know/** on your phone or computer, pick a section, tap **Sing**, and allow the microphone.

- Green: right note, on time
- Orange: right note, off time
- Red: wrong note, drawn where you sang it

You get a letter grade at the end. You can also grade a recording (for example a Voice Memo saved to Files) with **Grade a recording**.
